## Full description

A better airport trip starts before you leave.

JetSweep turns your flight details into a clear departure plan. Choose your airport,
add your travel details, and see when to start your journey—with room for the steps
that a driving estimate alone doesn't cover.

- Build a plan in three simple steps.
- Find nearby supported US airports with an optional location check.
- Save a default airport or reuse your most recent selection.
- Include security screening, checked bags, terminal walks, and rideshare pickup.
- Adjust for your travel party, busy travel periods, and preferred buffer.
- See the timing behind each stage of the journey.
- Reopen recent plans and update them for your next trip.
- Keep your recent plans on your device; no account is required.

JetSweep provides planning estimates. It does not provide live traffic, flight status,
security wait times, or airline check-in deadlines. All times use the device timezone
shown in the app. Check your airline's requirements and current travel conditions.
Car estimates cover curbside drop-off; parking and shuttle time are not included.
